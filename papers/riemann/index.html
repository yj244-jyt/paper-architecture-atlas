Type-0 · invariant scalars

\[f_0^{\,gP}(Qx+u)=f_0^{\,P}(x)\]

Color, saliency, and position weights move with the same object point as scalars; rotation does not turn them into vectors.

Position · normalized weights

\[\hat t'=\sum_i\alpha_i(Qx_i+u)=Q\hat t+u,\quad\sum_i\alpha_i=1\]

With invariant weights on the same target points, the weighted position rotates and translates with the object.

Type-1 · equivariant vectors

\[v_k'=Qv_k,\qquad \hat R_o'=Q\hat R_o\]

All three orientation axes rotate together; local pooling and orthogonalization preserve left multiplication by Q.

Paper §§3.1–4.1, pp.3–5. Interactions between target neighborhoods and distractors affect the trained model's local equivariance; these equations show the ideal construction.
