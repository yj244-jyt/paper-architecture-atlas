Read the limits

What this supports

On these tasks and this shared setup, video co-training contributes more to success than explicit future generation at inference. It does not establish that imagination is unnecessary for every task or model scale.

The paper's real-world no-video-co-training ablation reaches 10% success; the interactive scenes here are visual explanations, not reproductions of measured robot trajectories.
