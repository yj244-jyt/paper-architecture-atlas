Paper equation (1)

Conceptual reverse process

\[a^{k-1}=\alpha_k\left(a^k-\gamma_k\epsilon_\theta(a^k,k,v,q)\right)+\sigma_k\mathcal N(0,I)\]

ak is a noisy action sequence; v is the 3D feature; q is the robot pose; the schedule determines the coefficients and noise term.

Diffusion training objective

\[\mathcal L=\operatorname{MSE}\!\left(\epsilon^k,\epsilon_\theta(\bar\alpha_k a^0+\bar\beta_k\epsilon^k,k,v,q)\right)\]

This is the paper's Eq. (2), written in its noise-prediction notation. The reported implementation switches to sample prediction; the target there is the clean action sample. The paper does not give a separate sample-prediction equation.

Training: 1,000 epochs for MetaWorld; 3,000 epochs for other simulation and real tasks; batch size 128. Inputs and actions are independently scaled per dimension to [-1, 1].

Sources: Section III-C; Appendix A; Table VII.

Scope of the reconstruction

The policy backbone is the convolutional Diffusion Policy architecture cited by DP3. This page shows its role and reported input/output contract; it does not claim a layer-by-layer UNet specification from this paper. The separate Simple DP3 variant removes redundant UNet components (Appendix D).

Efficiency

DP3 reports 12.7 FPS and 74.4% mean success on the ten-task comparison. Simple DP3 reports 25.3 FPS and 70.2%. The speed comparison is tied to the paper's setup; it is not a universal hardware benchmark.

Appendix D, Table XVI.

Boundaries

Point clouds do not make the policy explicitly SE(3)-equivariant. The paper demonstrates empirical generalization across space, view, appearance, and object instances. The single-view design can still be limited by occlusion or unavailable depth.
